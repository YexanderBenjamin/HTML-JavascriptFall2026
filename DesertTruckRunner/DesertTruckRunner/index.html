How to Play

Move between the three lanes to avoid barricades and collect fuel.

  • Regular fuel cans add 100 fuel.
  • Gold fuel cans refill your tank to 250.
  • Your tank loses 10 fuel every 20 meters.
  • A bubble protects you from one barricade hit for a short time.
  • Reach 1,000 meters to win.
