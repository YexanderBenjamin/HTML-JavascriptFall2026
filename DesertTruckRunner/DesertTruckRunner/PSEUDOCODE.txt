DESERT TRUCK RUNNER - PSEUDOCODE
Group Members: Emmanuel and Yexander

GAME SETUP
- Set the canvas width to 800 and height to 500.
- Create three lanes: Left, Middle, and Right.
- Place the truck in the Middle lane near the bottom of the screen.
- Set the starting fuel to 250.
- Set the maximum fuel to 250.
- Set the distance traveled to 0 meters.
- Set the winning distance to 1,000 meters.
- Load the truck, barricade, regular gas, gold gas, and bubble images.

STARTING THE GAME
- Show the title screen and game instructions.
- Wait for the player to press Space.
- Reset the truck, fuel, distance, obstacles, and power-ups.
- Change the game state to Playing.

PLAYER MOVEMENT
- If the player presses A or the Left Arrow, move one lane left.
- If the player presses D or the Right Arrow, move one lane right.
- Do not let the truck move outside the three lanes.
- Keep the truck near the bottom of the screen.

DISTANCE AND FUEL
- Increase the distance at 10 meters per second.
- Every time the player travels another 20 meters, remove 10 fuel.
- Do not allow fuel to go above 250.
- If fuel reaches 0, end the game.

FUEL CONTAINERS
- Spawn a fuel container every 100 meters.
- Choose a random lane for the container.
- Most containers are regular red gas cans.
- Sometimes spawn a gold gas can instead.
- When the truck collects a regular gas can, add 100 fuel.
- When the truck collects a gold gas can, refill fuel to 250.
- Remove the collected fuel container from the screen.

BARRICADES
- Begin spawning barricades at 100 meters.
- Continue spawning barricades at regular distance intervals.
- Choose random lanes for barricades.
- Never block all three lanes at once; always leave at least one lane open.
- Move barricades toward the bottom of the screen.
- Use the GameObject overlaps() method to check whether the truck hits a barricade.
- If the truck hits a barricade without protection, end the game.

BUBBLE POWER-UP
- Spawn a bubble power-up occasionally.
- When the truck collects it, activate a temporary shield.
- The shield protects the truck from one barricade collision.
- Remove the shield after it blocks a collision or its timer runs out.

WINNING AND LOSING
- If fuel reaches 0, show the Game Over screen.
- If the truck hits a barricade without a shield, show the Game Over screen.
- If the player reaches 1,000 meters, show the Win screen.
- Allow the player to press Space to restart after winning or losing.

MAIN GAME LOOP
- Clear the previous frame.
- Draw the desert and road.
- Read player movement.
- Update distance and fuel.
- Spawn fuel cans, barricades, and power-ups when their distance targets are reached.
- Move objects down the screen.
- Check collisions with overlaps().
- Remove objects that leave the screen or are collected.
- Check win and game-over conditions.
- Draw the truck, objects, distance, fuel bar, and messages.
- Repeat about 60 times per second.


IMAGE SETUP (TEACHER'S REQUIRED METHOD)
- Keep the default Mr. T. image in index.html with id="default".
- Add each transparent PNG to index.html with its own id (avatar, barricade, gas, gold-gas, bubble).
- Create each object by passing its image ID into GameObject, such as new GameObject("#avatar").
- Set the object's img.w and img.h (or img.scale.x and img.scale.y) to control its displayed size.
- Draw image objects with the object's .graphic() method. Do not call drawImage() from game.js.
- The GameObject class uses the canvas drawing method internally to display the selected image.
